## Direction contract

THESIS: The real hand-lettered vinyl banner on the shop wall (outside.webp) IS the brand — the whole homepage is built as bold, blocky cut-vinyl sign-shop graphics, refusing the category's soft SaaS-gradient, icon-grid, rounded-pill tire-shop template.

OWN-WORLD: Committed color strategy: signal red (from the logo) owns 30-60% of the surface as flat ink blocks — hero band, section dividers, CTA panels — against white and warm concrete-gray grounds, with jet black for lettering and rules. Display type is Anton (tall, bold, even-stroke block lettering, reads like cut vinyl); body/UI type is Barlow (its own design story starts from American route-signage lettering, plain and legible at small sizes). Panels get angled, trimmed-corner edges evoking cut vinyl; hairline rules stand in for grommet lines; no gradients, no glass, no soft drop shadows.

STORY: A driver with an urgent tire/repair need or a budget-conscious tire shopper lands, immediately reads "real, open, fast, fair" from the banner-scale hero, and calls or books within seconds; a full-service + financing + bilingual signal reassures them this is one stop for the whole car and accessible regardless of credit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width vinyl-banner-style hero: giant stacked "BROTHERS TIRE" lettering in red/black over a white/concrete ground, the real logo mark set like a shop emblem, phone number set at road-sign scale, sticky header repeats phone + Book Now. One tightly scannable block below the banner packs hours, address, and the service list at a glance, no essential fact requiring a scroll to find. The real exterior photo (outside.webp) appears bled to an edge as proof-of-place, not as a soft background wash.

FORM: Shop-Sign Graphics, candidate 3 of 7 grounded directions ranked by resonance (Cinderblock Architecture, Roadside Billboard, Shop-Sign Graphics, Price/Specials Board, NASCAR/Pit-Crew Graphics, Service-Counter Queue, Tire-Stack Display); seed key c85ce8cc. Raise (donated from the declined Japanese high-density-web challenger): pack essential facts into one scannable module in the first viewport rather than spreading them down the scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
